Changelog — PulseCap

5.4.0 (2026-07-17)

  • Weekly coach report: volume per muscle vs target, weak-point flags, next-week advice
  • Plate calculator + warm-up ramp inside the active workout logger
  • Rest-timer system notifications when the app is backgrounded
  • Home-screen shortcuts: Today's workout / Start / Exercises
  • Offline form loops for the top-50 compounds (SVG cues when wger isn't cached)
  • Fixed skip-link overlay, gender icon clip, stale Settings version, Safari "Load failed" toast
  • VaultCap-style screen gallery with section filters

5.3.0 (2026-07-17)

  • Stronglifts, Starting Strength and 5/3/1 now run their real programs — exact sets prescribed, weights progress automatically, deloads handled
  • Build your own split: pick your days, search 300+ exercises, save — it becomes your plan
  • Dates now follow your timezone (streaks and schedules were off for evening lifters)
  • Injury filtering now covers every exercise in the library
  • More line icons, deeper body-map shading, smarter skip-day detection

5.2.0 (2026-07-16)

  • Light + dark themes only — follows your phone by default, pin either in Settings
  • Skip a day and the coach reshuffles your week — or tells you it costs nothing
  • Weigh-ins get a real answer: on pace, eat more, or watch the weekend
  • Weekly recap card, smarter streaks (rest days count, one freeze a week), morning check-in, private progress photos
  • New splash and welcome screens; app copy rewritten to sound like a coach, not a manual
  • Body tab redesigned — muscle recovery now respects your injuries, cleaner cards, one tools grid
  • High-definition body map — real muscle shapes, front and back, injury-aware colors
  • Swapping an exercise shows ranked alternatives with % match and a best pick
  • Floating glass tab bar, fixed properly — content always scrolls clear of it
  • New pulse-line app icon everywhere: home screen, splash, welcome
  • Body map is now your body — it reshapes with your gender, height, weight, body fat and muscle
  • Settings cleaned up: theme picker, coach personality and tone, macro presets
  • Cleaner, more professional look — crisp line icons replace emoji across the main screens
  • Every split checked end-to-end; all exercises now carry full coaching data
  • Fixed unreadable text in light mode and raw labels like "upper_chest" showing in the app

5.1.0 (2026-07-16)

  • Weekly schedule: split days auto-map to your gym days (Monday shows Monday's workout), editable per weekday in Settings → Training
  • Scheduled rest days with active-recovery guidance and a "Train anyway" option
  • Injury logging unified under Body → Rehab — severity, phase protocol, and workout adaptation in one place
  • Injury-aware workouts: unsafe exercises auto-swapped via avoid-lists and joint-stress ratings, "Modified for X" badge
  • Supplements due today surface on the dashboard
  • Bottom-sheet fix: close button, keyboard-safe height, background scroll lock (measurements modal)
  • New app icon propagated to iOS home-screen icons; SW pulsecap-v52
