PulseCap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

PulseCap is an offline-first fitness Progressive Web App. Most personal data is stored and processed in your browser, but some optional features use external services.

Data stored on your device

  • Profiles, settings, workouts, nutrition, measurements, training plans, and other app state are stored in browser localStorage.
  • Plan import (text PDF / JSON / pasted text) is processed in your browser. Files are not uploaded. PulseCap keeps the structured plan in localStorage after you confirm the review; the original file is not stored by default. Scanned / image-only PDFs are unsupported (no OCR).
  • Progress photos are stored in browser IndexedDB.
  • PulseCap has no account system and includes no analytics or advertising by default.

Network and device features

  • Optional exercise-library requests and media from wger disclose normal request data, such as your IP address and browser information, to wger and its infrastructure.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app, and Google Fonts may provide font files. Those providers receive normal web request data when your browser connects to them.
  • The optional barcode scanner requests camera access. Camera input is used to scan a barcode; permission remains controlled by your browser or device.
  • Optional voice logging uses the browser's SpeechRecognition service. Audio or transcripts may be processed by the browser vendor or another provider and are not guaranteed to remain offline.
  • Notifications and screen wake lock use browser or device permissions. PulseCap does not use them to create an account or track you.

Exports, retention, and deletion

  • Exports are created only when you request them. You control where exported files are saved or shared.
  • Local data remains until you delete it in PulseCap, reset the app, or clear the site's browser data. Uninstalling the PWA may not remove browser storage on every platform.
  • Reset and browser-data controls affect only data stored by PulseCap on that browser profile. They cannot delete exported copies or records retained by external providers under their own policies.
